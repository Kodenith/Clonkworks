#OFFICIAL CLONKWORKS CHANGELOG#

[??? 2026 - Version 1.1 Gaseous Update] < CURRENT!
* Added Gas - a new state of matter that can be used as hazards in levels or as weapons.
	- Includes Template Object to make your own Gas fast.
	- Gas Types: Toxic Fumes, Natural Gas.
* Added Geysers, new hazards that spew material or gas.
* Added Geyser Pumps, which pump out gas and liquid from geysers until theyre sucked dry.
	- Included are 2 Environment Rules to make extraction infinite!
* Added Gas Canisters - Barrels for gas.
* New Goal: Gas Extraction. Extract all geysers with Natural Gas, and then sell all canisters with the gas.
* Updated Goal: Oil Production now has similiar UI to the changed up oremine goals.
* Added the Hazmat Clonk. This Clonk Can stay in Toxic Fumes until his filters run out.
	- Included are rules to make filters not needed at all, and to make a craftable hazmat suit wearable by clonks.
	- Filters are craftable and purchasable
	- Low-res model taken from QuakeR. High-Res model was made by me (Kodenith) based on the Low-res model, and Ryou's HD Portrait Clonk Model.
* Updated Several Rule Graphics.
* Added the Humidifier - Basically Sprinkler for Gas.
* Humidifier and Sprinkler can now have their respective matter delivered to them via line kit.
* The Workspedia doesnt require an object to be researched in order to learn it. This used to not be the case.
* Added Autofiller, new conveyor station for barrels and gas.
