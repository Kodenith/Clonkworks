The Engineer can produce different items and vehicles on the go, and can hold an additional item.

Press Special 1 to change between items in the inventory.
Press Special 2 to Set or Remove a currently crafted item, add ingredient to current crafting, or begin crafting. The Infobar on the bottom left will tell you the progress of acquiring items.

<c ff0000>WARNING: Cancelling production leaves less than or half the items you gathered!</c>
