With the "Cavecrawler Cannons" Rule, Cavecrawlers can use cannons you'd typically use for the cannon tower. this lets you use it as a sort of portable cannon tower or an earthly alternative to the submarine :)

Each cavecrawler can only hold one cannon head, and the amount of ammo is limited by how much the vehicle fits. And any debri can damage it, be careful while handling cannons with the cavecrawler!
