The Clonk species attempts to replicate the wildlife of their world as a reliable exploration tool.

Instructions:
Fill this vehicle with plenty of oil barrels, as it consumes a lot of fuel. Then you can drive it by pressing the up button in front of the vehicle. You can walk, jump, or do a side jump in the air by pressing the left or right button several times. Jumping and side jumps consume additional fuel. You can exit the vehicle by pressing the down button twice.
